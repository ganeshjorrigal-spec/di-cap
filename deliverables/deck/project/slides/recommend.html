Revenue growth above 6% a year

About 35 seconds. So I back Dabur, but only while three things stay true. One: the operating margin stops sliding. It was 18.4% in FY22 and 15.0% in FY26; it has to hold at 15% or better. Two: revenue has to grow faster than the 4.9% a year it has managed since FY22, above 6%. Three: the 9,489 crore of cash and investments, more than half its balance sheet, has to be used or returned. Today a quarter of its profit before tax is interest on that cash.
