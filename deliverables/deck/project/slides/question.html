The question, and the one difference

Which business would come through a bad year better, judged on how it makes money, turns profit into cash, earns returns and carries risk?

Dabur's profit becomes cash, and its suppliers fund its working capital.

Marico earns more on its capital today. But its FY26 numbers lean on copra prices and newly bought brands, and its cash has not kept pace with its profit every year.

Consolidated statements, FY24 to FY26. Not a view on either share price.

About 40 seconds. I tracked Dabur and compared it with Marico. Both are Indian consumer companies with net cash and very little debt. Marico earns more on its capital; I will not pretend otherwise. But the question is which is the stronger and more resilient business. The one difference that decides it for me: Dabur's profit turns into cash, and its suppliers, not its shareholders, fund its working capital. Marico's latest year leans on copra prices and on brands it has just bought.
