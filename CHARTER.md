# CHARTER: Dabur vs Marico capstone

Rewriting this file is a decision (log it in DECISIONS.md), not an edit.

## Goal
Answer one question with evidence: on business economics, cash generation, returns and financial risk, which is the stronger, more resilient business, Dabur India or Marico, and what would change my mind?

"Back" means stronger business, not a buy/sell call on the shares. Share price may appear only as a limitation.

## Commissioner and reviewers
- Course: Foundation of Finance (Business Finance), PGPM, Crucible Institute of Management. Individual work.
- Marked by the instructor. 25 of 100 course marks: presentation 10, written submission 10, reflection 5.
- Project reviewer for milestones: Ganesh. Cold reviewer: Gemini 2.5 Pro.

## Deliverables and definition of done
1. **Deck PDF**: title + max 4 slides, 3 minutes hard stop, max one table per slide (5 rows), every number sourced at the bottom, no paragraphs. Target: 4 Nov 2026.
2. **Written submission PDF**: Part A Dabur (elements 1 to 6, max 3 pages), Part B Marico (element 7, 1 page), Part C argument (elements 8 and 9, max 2 pages), numbers table (every figure, Reported / Calculated / Assumed).
3. **Personal reflection**: one page, three questions, from Ganesh's own answers.
4. **Q&A prep**: likely instructor questions with the source of each number.

Done = every item on the brief's "Before you submit" checklist passes, Gemini review answered, Ganesh ticks DONE.

## Constraints
- Consolidated statements; FY24 to FY26 as the core years for both; five years (FY22 to FY26) where a trend is required.
- Same definitions for both companies; definitions stated in one line.
- DuPont on closing balances and must tie. CCC components must add, and year-on-year changes must add.
- Indian statutory P&L has no COGS line: gross margin built as revenue minus (materials consumed + purchases of stock-in-trade + change in inventories), stated as such.
- No invented numbers. Gaps stated with where we looked.
- Course material is context only and never referenced in the output.

## What this is not
- Not a valuation or share-price recommendation.
- Not new research beyond what the argument needs.
- Not a group deliverable.
